Normalized 3NF Relational Database Schema

8 Normalized Entities with Crow's Foot Relational Constraints (`ssis_schema.sql`).

Normalization Compliance Summary:

✓ 1NF: Atomic columns, unique primary keys, zero repeating attribute groups.
✓ 2NF: Full functional dependency on candidate keys; no partial composite key dependencies.
✓ 3NF: Zero transitive non-key dependencies. Department details, Course offerings, and Clearance states isolated into dedicated relation tables.

1. Department

(PK: DepartmentID)

Colleges and operational university offices.

2. Student

(PK: StudentID, UQ: Email)

Student credentials, program, address, bcrypt hash.

3. AdminUser

(PK: AdminID, FK: DepartmentID)

Institutional staff with role-based access.

4. Course

(PK: CourseID, FK: DepartmentID)

Academic curriculum subjects & credit units.

5. Enrollment

(PK: EnrollmentID, FK: StudentID, CourseID)

Many-to-many link resolving grades and terms.

6. Payment

(PK: PaymentID, FK: StudentID, UQ: ORNumber)

Official receipt counter cashier transactions.

7. DocumentRequest

(PK: RequestID, FK: StudentID, AdminID)

Fulfillment lifecycle of TOR, COR, and Certifications.

8. Clearance

(PK: ClearanceID, FK: StudentID, DepartmentID)

Student-to-department clearance hold/clear records.
